Our Story

School History

Pundibari R.G.L High School was established in {{ settings.established }} with a vision to bring quality education to Pundibari and surrounding areas of Cooch Behar district, West Bengal.

Over the decades, the school has grown into one of the region's most respected government aided schools, serving students from Class 5 through Class 12.

Affiliated with WBBSE and WBCHSE, our school maintains consistently high academic standards while nurturing holistic development.
